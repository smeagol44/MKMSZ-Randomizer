### PS-17 (2026-11-06)

**2026-11-06 vanilla audio FIFO speed-up — root cause found; production fix Runtime-confirmed bounded.** Each VI, `0x8007D4A8` first submits the previous PCM buffer to the two-slot AI FIFO (`0x8008A500`; full returns `-1` and the buffer is dropped), then reads `AI_LEN` (head remainder only) and sizes the next buffer as `max(352, ((368 - (AI_LEN >> 2) + reserve) & ~15) + 16)`. Target 368 and minimum 352 are derived at audio init from the DAC rate and refresh; the maximum is `target + reserve + 16`. The reserve (320) is written into the boot audio config by one immediate, `addiu v0,zero,0x140` at VA `0x80001354` / ROM `0x1F54` (caller `0x800012EC` → config apply `0x8007DA60`, flags `0x16B` with bit `0x8`, field `+0x10`). That keeps the FIFO nearly full with only about 1–3 ms of headroom: whenever the next submission still finds both slots occupied, the buffer is dropped while the synthesizer and WESS sequence have already advanced, so music runs ahead and effects crackle. Under RMG's integer AI timebase a 368-sample buffer is 20 Count shorter than one VI, so the loop drifts across its 16-sample steps very slowly and lingers near that edge, where tiny jitter tips it into bursts. An exact closed-loop model of this rule (two-slot AI FIFO, pinned RMG timing of 551 Count/byte and 811,092 Count/VI, measured service jitter of about ±400 Count) reproduces every regime in the existing traces: healthy 368-sample plateaus, the 352-sample "slowdown" lock, isolated rejections roughly every 30 s (as in vanilla Trace 8), and random, minutes-long rejection bursts ("speed-up"). In the same model, reserve 288 or lower removes every burst with no AI starvation, including other AI/VI ratios and ±20,000-Count jitter; an extreme artificial case (1% of services up to a quarter-frame late) still drops some buffers at every reserve, fewer at lower ones. **Runtime evidence (maintainer, 2026-11-06):** the speed-up reproduced in the **unmodified vanilla USA Rev. 0 ROM** while idling at the password screen. Proof `MKMSZR_audio-reserve-272_all-stages_proof_v01.z64` (production build of `main` `4646a69`, seed `AUDIORESERVE01`, MKT donor, plus reserve 320 → 272; SHA-256 `47da43ef6f3dddf11e7652c60d94fe3ee5c4aa3740ce5f230c0553b4fe077740`) ran the same idle scenario plus the usual triggers without any speed-up. The maintainer heard occasional very short "pops", at worst about every 20 s and sometimes none for minutes, and judged them acceptable; their source (model gap, host audio, or pre-existing) is Pending. Production now lowers the reserve to 272 by default (`AudioFifoReservePatch`, ROM `0x1F54`), with a debug opt-out. Details: [Audio system](Sounds-and-Music).
